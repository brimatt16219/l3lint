# VISA-ELIG-004: Federal government card at an excluded medical MCC

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Severity | information |
| Confidence | processor-doc |
| Level | general |
| Brand | visa |
| Impact | noBenefit |
| Effective | from 2025-10-17 |
| Pack | l3lint.rules.visa 1.0.0 |

## Requirement

Federal government corporate and purchasing cards are excluded from Product 3 at medical MCCs.

## Why

Visa's exclusion for federal cards covers doctors, dentists, hospitals and similar MCCs.

## Check

`inList /merchant/mcc (list: visaFederalExcludedMcc; mode: deny; caseInsensitive: false; allowMissing: true)`

## Applies to

- only when /card/isFederalGovernment equals true

## Message

`Federal government cards are excluded from Product 3 at MCC {actual}.`

## Fix

None.

## Sources

- <https://docs.stripe.com/payments/payment-line-items>
