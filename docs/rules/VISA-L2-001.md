# VISA-L2-001: Tax outside 0.1% to 22% of the amount

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Severity | error |
| Confidence | processor-doc |
| Level | L2 |
| Brand | visa |
| Impact | blocksLevel2 |
| Effective | until 2026-04-17 (last day the rule applies) |
| Pack | l3lint.rules.visa 1.0.0 |

## Requirement

Until the Level 2 sunset, Visa Level 2 needed tax between 0.1% and 22% of the amount, except at fuel MCCs or when the sale is exempt.

## Why

Tax outside the range was treated as not real, so the transaction settled without the Level 2 rate.

## Check

`ratio /tax/amount, /amount (min: 0.001; max: 0.22)`

## Applies to

- card products: business, corporate, purchasing, fleet
- skipped when the MCC is on: visaFuelMcc
- skipped when /tax/indicator equals exempt

## Message

`Tax is {ratio} of the amount; Visa Level 2 needs {min} to {max}.`

## Fix

Correct the tax amount or mark the sale exempt.

## Sources

- <https://developer.paypal.com/braintree/docs/reference/general/level-2-and-3-processing/required-fields/dotnet/>
- <https://docs.stripe.com/payments/payment-line-items>

## Notes

Sources give April 17 and April 18, 2026 for the Level 2 sunset; the pack uses April 17 as the last day these rules apply (the stricter reading, risk R2).
